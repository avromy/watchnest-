## Optional concerns — not material failures

1. Parent timer/discovery management is a long repeated-card page. At 768×1024, `parent-controls-top.png`, `parent-controls-middle-1.png` and `parent-controls-bottom.png` show the distance from session actions to discovery and available-hours settings. On phone this can require substantial scrolling for a lower child. The grouping is clear, but a child selector or section jump could improve repeated administration.
2. In `390x844/parent-details/parent-approval-assignment-middle-1.png` and `.../parent-approval-assignment-bottom.png`, assignment and approval sit above the selected result. This is understandable for the one-result fixture, though larger result sets may require repeated travel between selection and approval. Larger realistic curation sets were not inspected.
3. In `1024x768/parent-preview-dialog-internal-bottom-full.png`, the internally scrolled modal bottom has no visible Close preview action; it is at the top in the phone preview capture. Keeping Close available while scrolling would reduce return travel. Screenshots alone do not establish an inability to dismiss with keyboard or backdrop.
4. The single Collection is displayed as a very wide card on larger screens (`1180x820/supplemental-responsive/child-library-top.png` and desktop). This adds vertical browsing distance before All videos. It is coherent and not broken, but the initial content density is deliberately low.
