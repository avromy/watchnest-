## Product decisions supported; no final design vote

1. Approved content is an actual library: persistent retrieval, search, Favorites,
   Collections and deterministic sorting matter more than a bigger promotional grid.
2. Profile identity must stay obvious before and during browsing. Profile switching
   is different from catalog filtering; protect Parent transitions separately.
3. Home earns its place only through a distinct useful resume/recent task. Library
   remains the complete retrieval surface. Empty and Simple modes must not add a
   welcome step solely to retain a design concept.
4. Parent administration is a recurring work surface. Counts and requests should
   precede acquisition marketing; existing Add videos remains a useful primary action.
5. Large artwork is appropriate for choosing content, not a reason to push Parent
   work below a sales hero. Touch layout, labels and focused selection are tested
   independently at portrait, landscape and phone widths.
6. External discovery remains a non-playable request. No competitor recommendation
   or engagement mechanism is transferred into Child Mode or the protected player.

Three prospective IA/layout alternatives are separately specified in
PremiumDirectionComparison-20261110.md. Their independent selection must be recorded
