## Judgment

The visible product succeeds as a warm, premium family library. Cream backgrounds, restrained forest green, muted artwork, rounded cards and consistent line icons form a coherent system. The typography is readable, headings establish a clear hierarchy, and spacing groups tasks effectively. Primary actions generally have substantial button surfaces and clear labels. Visible focused PIN and search fields have conspicuous gold outlines.

Child navigation is understandable: Home, Library and Discover remain clear and consistently selected; phone navigation moves to a persistent bottom bar. Search, favorites and collection return paths are visible. Approved-library browsing is visually distinct from asking Parent to add discoveries. The hero's Keep watching action is explicit. Native phone middle/bottom captures show that final library items and request actions can appear above the bottom navigation; the overlay visible in full-document screenshots is not by itself evidence of unreachable content.

Parent navigation divides curation from family administration cleanly. The dashboard prioritizes pending requests; library cards distinguish Preview, Manage and Remove. Assignment exposes specific children and an optional collection before approval. Collection editing, PIN management and session controls use recognizable forms and plain action labels. Active session controls visually distinguish End now from Pause and Add time.

Both primary iPad orientations look composed. Phone forms stack without clipped labels or horizontal collisions in the viewed images. Supplemental widths from 360 through 1280 pixels show sensible transitions; desktop preserves hierarchy and readable controls. Error, empty, PIN, paused, expired and ended examples provide understandable messages and recovery actions.

**Supported material visual defects: none found in the consumed sample.**

## Optional concerns — not failure findings

- Density can improve. In `1024x768/child-library-top.png`, the header, search/filter area and approximately 190-pixel collection card consume the entire initial viewport before individual videos appear. The similarly expansive desktop collection tile in `1440x1000/child-library-top.png` has substantial unused area. This adds scrolling but preserves a clear structure.
- The Parent approval panel precedes result cards. In `390x844/parent-details/parent-url-resolved-full.png`, a first-time Parent must scroll through assignment controls before selecting the video below, then return to approve. “Choose videos below” helps; a closer selected-video/action relationship could reduce friction.
- “Ask Parent another way” appears as the only request mechanism when discovery is disabled (`768x1024/child-details/child-discover-disabled-success-full.png`). A simpler label would better match that state.
- The manifest reports a 119 × 22-pixel “Manage family” link on tablet/desktop. The visible link is secondary and an alternative Family navigation button exists, so I do not treat this as a material blocker. Checkbox element dimensions alone do not establish the size of their associated clickable labels.
- Some administration pages are long, particularly four-child controls and protection guidance. Their grouping remains clear, but collapsible child sections or progressive guidance could reduce scanning effort.

## Boundaries

All other images remain unreviewed by this juror. Channel/playlist result variants, every loading state, all success variants, profile-photo alternatives, and every timer connectivity combination were not individually inspected. Supplemental widths were sampled rather than exhaustively reviewed.

Manifest geometry was used only as supporting context; it is not substitute pixel acceptance. I did not perform keyboard traversal, screen-reader testing, measured contrast testing, touch hit-testing or functional QA. Focus visibility is assessed only where shown. Synthetic artwork does not establish real-thumbnail behavior or arbitrary real-world text robustness.

No acceptance is claimed for hosted or authenticated configuration, provider/YouTube behavior, genuine playback, physical iPad/Safari, native fullscreen, remote picture/sound stopping, security, authorization, sibling isolation, persistence, or deployed-browser operation. Product statements about these behaviors in the images remain unverified by this review.
