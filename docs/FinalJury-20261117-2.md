## Independence and method

I read the neutral review index, its product brief and source binding, and metadata from the 26 referenced capture manifests. All 26 manifest bindings matched the candidate and tree. The index contains 931 images and 293 capture states. This is a substantial sampled visual review, not a claim that every indexed image was inspected. Metadata was supporting navigation/binding evidence, not a substitute for pixels.

## Decision and supported findings

No material visual/usability defect was established in the images consumed.

The warm ivory, deep green and restrained gold system coheres across child and Parent surfaces. Typography is readable, with a clear hierarchy and consistent rounded components. Icons form a consistent family. Parent navigation separates Overview, Library, Requests and Family; the secondary navigation makes collection, curation and time-management tasks discoverable. Adding a video, assigning children and approving has visible labels and a clear primary action. Destructive timer ending is visually distinct from extending or pausing.

The child interface gives large, understandable navigation and generous image targets. Approved-library browsing, Favorites, Collections, search and asking Parent are visually differentiated. Discover results offer an Ask Parent action rather than implying immediate playback. Empty, error, paused and expired states give legible next steps. The PIN dialogs make the subject/profile clear and show conspicuous focus/error treatment.

The iPad portrait and landscape samples remain composed and readable at the top and deeper document positions. Long titles wrap without colliding with favorite or overflow controls. Phone layouts deliberately move child navigation to a fixed bottom dock; the inspected final content remains above it. Parent cards and forms reflow into a usable single column. Desktop library images show stable three-column alignment and clear per-card controls. Stress samples at widths 360, 412, 520, 640, 820, 900, 1180 and 1280 show coherent adaptation in the inspected regions.

Visual contrast appears adequate for primary text/actions and focus rings in the sampled images. This is an eye review, not measured WCAG certification. The manifests flag some small individual checkbox/input boxes and text links; the visible containing labels/cards often provide substantially larger apparent selection areas. I did not independently interact to verify effective hit areas, so I do not certify all touch targets from those inventories.

## Optional concerns — do not change this verdict

- Library entry is relatively spacious: in `1024x768/child-library-top.png` the single Collection card plus heading/search/filter region occupies almost the entire first viewport, pushing individual videos below it. This remains understandable and navigable, but reducing the vertical cost when there is only one Collection could make frequent browsing faster.
- Parent time/profile administration is long on phone. `390x844/parent-children-middle-1.png` and `390x844/parent-details/parent-remote-timer-active-middle-1.png` show repeated per-child forms and explanatory copy. The grouping is clear; a future product refinement could improve rapid access to a particular child's controls.
- `390x844/parent-password-recovery-full.png` has no visible email field or recipient cue. Images alone do not reveal whether a previous step supplies the address. Confirm that the complete recovery journey makes the destination understandable. This is an unverified workflow question, not an established failure from the static capture.

## Honest boundaries

This verdict covers the rendered synthetic samples listed below only. I did not conduct live interaction, keyboard traversal, screen-reader testing, measured color contrast, zoom/text enlargement, physical touch-target testing or a complete accessibility audit. No hosted authentication, genuine provider playback, real remote audio/video stopping, physical iPad/Safari/PWA/native fullscreen, security, sibling isolation or approved-only authorization acceptance is claimed. Synthetic preview blocks are not playback proof.

I did not visually inspect every empty/loading/error/success state at every viewport, every middle tile of long pages, all channel/playlist result variants, all photo/avatar choices, or every player authorization/next-video variant. Unlisted images are unreviewed by this juror. Those limitations must remain attached to this PASS.
